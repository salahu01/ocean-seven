02 — Cold chain

Five stops. Five stops.
Never warm. Never warm.

Freshness is decided by temperature, not by promises. Here is the route every order takes.

  1. Stop 01

    Source

    Local boats on the Red Sea and vetted producers abroad. Every lot is traceable to where it came from.

    Iced at landing
  2. Stop 02

    Inspect

    Eyes, gills, smell and core temperature are checked on arrival. Anything that fails goes back.

    Core ≤ 4 °C
  3. Stop 03

    Chill or freeze

    Fresh product stays on ice. Imported product arrives frozen and is never allowed to thaw.

    0 – 4 °C / −18 °C
  4. Stop 04

    Store

    Held in temperature-controlled storage in Jeddah, close to the Islamic Port, first in, first out.

    Logged daily
  5. Stop 05

    Deliver

    Refrigerated delivery to your kitchen, store or warehouse, at the temperature it was stored at.

    Door at 0 – 4 °C / −18 °C

01

What is Ocean Seven?

Ocean Seven Seafood Trading Company (شركة أوشن سيفن سيفود تريدينغ) is a seafood wholesaler based in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. We source fresh Red Sea fish, import frozen seafood and supply ready-to-cook seafood products to food businesses across the Kingdom.

02

Where are you located?

Building 6686, Khalid Ibn Al Waleed Street, Ash Sharafiyah District, Jeddah 23216. Our Saudi National Address short code is JBSA6686.

03

Who do you supply?

Hotels, restaurants and cafés; supermarkets and fish retailers; caterers and institutional kitchens; and wholesalers and distributors.

04

How do I get a price list?

Use the request form or email info@oceanseven.sa with your business type, city and what you need. Seafood prices move with the market, so we reply with current prices and availability rather than publishing a fixed list.

05

Can you source something you don’t list?

Yes. The range above is our regular programme. Tell us the species, cut, size grade, pack format and expected volume and we will source to order.

06

What temperatures do you keep seafood at?

Chilled seafood is held between 0 °C and 4 °C. Frozen seafood is stored and transported at −18 °C or colder, from intake to delivery.
